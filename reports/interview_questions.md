# 10 Interview Questions
1. What is an IDS? A system that monitors activity and alerts on suspicious behavior.
2. Why synthetic traffic? It is safe, repeatable and needs no real infrastructure.
3. What is signature detection? Predefined pattern or threshold detection.
4. What is anomaly detection? Detecting deviation from a baseline.
5. Why hybrid detection? Different methods provide complementary evidence.
6. What is risk score? A 0-100 investigation-priority score.
7. Is a rule match proof of attack? No; it requires context and investigation.
8. What is a false positive? Benign activity incorrectly flagged.
9. Why optional ML? Explainable detection can work without ML.
10. How make it production-ready? Add authentication, streaming, calibrated models, SIEM integration and secure deployment.
